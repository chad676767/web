Why Are Oceans Important🌊

Oceans cover most of Earth’s surface and are home to millions of plants and animals. They provide food, oxygen, and help control the Earth’s temperature. Oceans are also important for people because they provide jobs, transport, tourism, and resources.

Amazing Ocean Animals🐠

The ocean is home to incredible animals such as whales, dolphins, sharks, sea turtles, penguins, and colourful fish. Some animals live near the surface, while others live deep underwater where there is almost no sunlight. Coral reefs are also home to thousands of different species.

The Problem of Ocean Pollution🗑️

Ocean pollution is a major problem. Plastic bags, bottles, fishing equipment, and other rubbish can end up in the water. Pollution can hurt or kill marine animals and damage important habitats such as coral reefs. Chemicals and oil can also make ocean water unsafe for wildlife.

Climate Change and the Ocean🌡️

Climate change is affecting the oceans by making the water warmer and causing sea levels to rise. Warmer water can damage coral reefs and affect where some animals can live. Oceans also absorb a lot of the extra heat and carbon dioxide produced by human activities.
How Can We Help?♻️
There are many things we can do to protect the ocean. We can use less plastic, recycle rubbish properly, use reusable bottles and bags, and never leave litter at beaches. We can also save electricity and water, choose sustainable seafood, and help clean up local beaches.
Protecting Our Future🌎
Everyone can play a part in protecting the ocean. Small actions, when millions of people do them, can make a big difference. By reducing pollution, protecting marine animals.
